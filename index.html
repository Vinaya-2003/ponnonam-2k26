Good to Know

  • Cultural events are organized company-wise so each team can prepare and practice within a short period.
  • Afternoon games are conducted by mixing everyone into different teams, to encourage interaction, teamwork, and participation.
  • All programs will be under the control of the Executive Committee.
  • Please be alert to maintain time management for clear execution of the programs.
  • Water bottles will be provided in the Seminar Hall and Centre Courtyard.
  • In case of any casualty, first aid will be provided.
  • Dressing rooms are provided at the venue:
    • Male: Cozmek DIP Room, 4th Floor
    • Female: Studio, 3rd Floor
